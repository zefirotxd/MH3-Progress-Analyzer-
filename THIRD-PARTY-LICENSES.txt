Optional remote visual references

Monster icons and armor-set reference images are requested at runtime from the
Monster Hunter Wiki on Fandom:
https://monsterhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Category:MH3_Monster_Icons
https://monsterhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Category:MH3_Armor

The icon category lists 35 Monster Hunter Tri icons and notes community
content is CC-BY-SA unless otherwise stated. The armor category credits
Bobofango for captured/scanned images and carries the same general notice.
These image files are not bundled or redistributed by this project. The source
pages identify their individual contributors and applicable terms. Images may
be unavailable when offline or when the source changes its file URLs.

Gathering guide data

Area and point tables are a normalized reference to the community guide at:
https://game.daihouko.com/mh/3/
Material-name cross-reference:
https://monsterhunter.fandom.com/wiki/MH3:_Item_List

The app includes only the tabulated map, area, point type, rank and possible
items. It does not claim in-area coordinates or per-item probability.
